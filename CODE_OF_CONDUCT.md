# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive and inclusive in project spaces and when
representing the project. Welcome different experience levels and backgrounds.
Critique ideas and code, accept correction, and respect privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption and publication of private information without
permission are unacceptable. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions
and other project communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Send confidential conduct reports to **alvit.work@gmail.com**. Include relevant
context and links; do not post personal information in a public issue. The
primary maintainer reviews reports, limits sharing to what is needed to respond,
and aims to acknowledge reports within 14 days. Absolute confidentiality cannot
be promised where disclosure is required by law or needed to address a threat.

Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include clarification, a
warning, removing content, temporary restrictions or removal from project spaces.
Decisions should explain the applicable behavior and proportionate response.
The reporter or affected participant can request reconsideration privately.

This is currently a single-maintainer project with no independent moderator or
appeal body. If a complaint concerns that maintainer, this limitation remains;
GitHub's own abuse-reporting process is available for conduct on its platform.
